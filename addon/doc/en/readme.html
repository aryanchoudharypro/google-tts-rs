Google TTS RS

Google TTS RS gives NVDA the Google WASM text-to-speech voices, including the high quality "Natural" ones. The speech engine runs as a small native program bundled with the add-on, so no web browser and no Node.js are involved.

Getting voices

No voices are included with the add-on; you download the ones you want. Open the voice manager from the NVDA menu, under Tools, or press NVDA+control+shift+R. The Download tab lists every available language pack, and the Installed tab lets you remove packs you no longer need.

Once at least one pack is installed, choose Google TTS RS as your synthesizer in NVDA's Speech settings, then pick a voice.

Natural voices

Voices labelled "Natural" come from a separate, smaller pack that builds on the standard pack for the same language. The voice manager downloads the standard pack automatically when you pick a Natural one, so there is nothing extra to do. If you remove a standard pack, the Natural voices for that language disappear from the voice list until you install it again.

Settings

Rate, pitch and volume work as they do with any NVDA synthesizer. Rate boost extends the top of the rate range for faster reading.

Disk space

Voice packs are large, from a few megabytes for a Natural pack to around twenty megabytes for a standard one. They are stored in your NVDA user configuration folder, under googleTtsRs\voices. Packs already downloaded by the Google TTS For NVDA add-on are reused automatically, so installing both add-ons does not download anything twice.
